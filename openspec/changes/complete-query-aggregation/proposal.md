# Proposal

## Why

Implausible publication years distort the time axis in CORE search analytics.

## What Changes

Group returned outlier year buckets under Unknown year and apply the same year cutoff on paper cards and date filters. The selectable-work-analytics change supersedes the original coverage investigation.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `publication-year-coverage` — normalize implausible years in the aggregate and raw responses independently.

## Impact

Publication-year normalization and its tests.
